## Frozen facade textures (assets/textures/karakol/fac_*.jpg, gf_*.jpg, dec_*.jpg, tower GLB facade image)

Derived from the facade photos inside "street city (7) for games FREE" (dasy444, Sketchfab Standard licence -
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/street-city-7-for-games-free-493a69b451284ff88346c7b3e4e1b5a7): cropped to whole
window bays / storeys, made seamless, cold-graded, with soot streaks, hoarfrost, frosted glass, snow on sills
and ledges and icicles added (scratchpad pipeline ice.py). Used inside the game only.

The ground snow (snow_*.jpg), wall masonry (wall_*.jpg) and trim (trim_stone.jpg) textures are original
procedural art made for Wolf Strike.
